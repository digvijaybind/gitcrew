# Plan

Read `BRIEF.md` (from the CEO) and `PROJECT.md` (the raw brief). Produce:

1. `PRD.md` — problem, users, experience story, sections (3-6), content plan,
   out of scope.
2. `TASKS.md` — ordered checklist grouped by build order, plus a `## Future`
   section.

Rules: every task is verifiable, ordered for the engineer, and scope stays
inside the CEO's boundaries. Commit as `plan: ...`.
